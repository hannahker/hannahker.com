I’m a Data Scientist at UN OCHA’s [Centre for Humanitarian Data](https://centre.humdata.org/). I’ve worked on systems for automated alerting on key indicators of humanitarian interest, frameworks for anticipating flooding in Nigeria, and internal indicators on accountability.

I previously led a team of developers at [Plotly](https://dash.plotly.com/) to help clients build data-driven web apps. Project highlights included building an [image segmentation interface](https://github.com/mlexchange/mlex_highres_segmentation) to help [scientists](https://als.lbl.gov/) understand the molecular structure of matter, and developing new visualization capabilities in Plotly's [JavaScript graphing library](https://github.com/plotly/plotly.js) to [support the work of electric vehicle engineers](https://twitter.com/plotlygraphs/status/1692239116740534662/photo/1).

I have a MSc in Spatial Data Science and Visualization from UCL's [Centre for Advanced Spatial Analysis](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/bartlett/casa/) and a BA in Urban Geography from McGill University.
